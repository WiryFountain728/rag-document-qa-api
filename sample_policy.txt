Aetheria Tech - Remote Work & Leave Policy

1. Working Hours & Core Time
All employees at Aetheria Tech are expected to be available during core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST. Standard working hours are 40 hours per week.

2. Health & Wellness Allowance
Each full-time employee receives an annual wellness stipend of $1,200. This stipend can be used for gym memberships, home ergonomics equipment, or mental health applications. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal by December 15th.

3. Annual Leave Policy
Full-time staff receive 20 paid vacation days annually. Vacation requests longer than 3 consecutive days require approval from your manager at least two weeks in advance. Unused vacation days do not roll over to the next calendar year.

4. Hardware Refresh Policy
Every software engineer receives a laptop and two 27-inch 4K monitors upon joining. Hardware upgrades occur automatically every 3 years.
